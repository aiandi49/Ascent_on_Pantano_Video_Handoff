Your 4-week content plan — ready to post

Social Media Campaign Calendar

Instagram · TikTok · Facebook · X  |  June 2026 · Produced by Lamar Myers

This calendar covers 4 weeks of content across all four platforms using your 8 professional videos. Each post includes a ready-to-publish caption and hashtag set — just tap Copy Caption or Copy Hashtags, then tap Download Video (this opens the video on Vimeo — tap the download/arrow icon there to save it to your phone or desktop). Once it's saved, open Instagram/TikTok/Facebook, start a new post, select that video from your gallery, and paste in the caption and hashtags. Posting tip: one post per day keeps your feed active and your algorithm happy.
First time downloading? On your phone, tap Download Video → tap the down-arrow icon on the Vimeo page → the video saves to your Photos/Gallery. On a computer, it saves to your Downloads folder — just drag that file into Instagram, TikTok, or Facebook when creating your post. It feels a little clunky the first time, but once you've done it once it becomes second nature.
